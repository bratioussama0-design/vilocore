// FAQs

Questions buyers ask first.

How do I install the Website Concierge?

Paste one script tag into your site, in your Shopify theme, WordPress header or custom code. That is the whole installation. The widget appears on your pages straight away.

Do you need my passwords or admin access?

No. Installation is a single script tag, so you never share a password. Tools like Google Sheets connect through a sheet you choose to share, never your login.

How long does setup take?

The Starter Concierge is typically live within a few days. Pro and the Sales & Orders plans usually take about a week. Enterprise plans and Custom AI Agents are scoped on a short call and take one to a few weeks. You get a timeline before we start.

How does the Website Concierge work?

It is trained on your pages, FAQs, policies and catalog. It answers visitors, recommends products, qualifies leads and sends the details to your Google Sheet or CRM, with instant alerts on high-intent visitors.

How does the Sales & Orders Engine work?

It pulls order details from your forms and chat, validates them, logs them in Google Sheets or Airtable and emails the buyer a confirmation. Higher tiers add follow-ups for abandoned orders, daily reports and two-way sync with your CRM, inventory and invoicing.

How do Custom AI Agents work?

We build n8n AI agents for the workflows you choose, such as lead enrichment, inbox triage, outreach drafting or a knowledge base over your docs. They connect to your existing tools and write structured results where your team already works.

Do I need WhatsApp or Meta accounts?

No. Everything runs through your website, n8n, and Google Sheets, your CRM and email. There are no WhatsApp or Meta dependencies.
